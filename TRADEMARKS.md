# The name and the logo

The code in this repository is free to use, change and share under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The names **Velocity** and **Velocity CMS** and the Velocity logo are not covered by that licence. They belong to Southwell Media.

You may:

- say that your site, plugin, template or service is built with, works with or is for Velocity CMS
- use the name to refer to this project, in articles, talks and documentation

Please do not:

- call a fork, a changed version or a hosted service "Velocity CMS", or give it a name or logo that could be taken for this project
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests the maintainers made or endorse your product

If you distribute a changed version, give it its own name. If you are unsure, open an issue and ask.
