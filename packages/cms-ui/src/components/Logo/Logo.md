# Logo

The Velocity mark. Its top is the brand colour and the rest follows the text colour, so it reads
on light and dark alike. On the editor's rail, which is dark in both themes, give it `tone="rail"`.

## Use it for

- The top of the rail and the sign-in screen, beside the site's name.

## Don't use it for

- Decoration, or anywhere it would appear twice on one screen.
- A site's own logo: that is the site's to provide.

## Example

```tsx
<Logo label="Velocity" />
<Logo label="Velocity" tone="rail" />
```
