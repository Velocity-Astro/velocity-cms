# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and make room for people who are new. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted here, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project meets.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here troubles you, tell the maintainers privately through a [private report](https://github.com/Velocity-Astro/velocity-cms/security/advisories/new) on this repository, marked as a conduct report. Reports are read by the maintainers only and are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may ban, for a time or for good, someone who keeps breaking it.
